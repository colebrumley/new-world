Every key and mouse action in the game, screen by screen.

## Playing with the mouse

You do not need the keyboard. The sidebar beside the map has a button for every order a unit can be given, and for End turn, Europe, zoom, the reports and the menus. Click a unit to make it the active one. Click the square beside it to move it there, or drag it to a square farther off to send it there. On the colony and Europe screens, everything can be clicked or dragged. Right-click anything to look it up in the encyclopedia ([[Help and Options]]).

The keyboard is quicker once you know it. The tables below list every key, and every mouse action, for each screen. Hold the pointer over a sidebar button to see its key.

## Notes

- **Moving.** The arrow keys move in four directions; the numeric keypad (or Home, End, Page Up and Page Down) also moves diagonally. A unit that runs out of moves waits for the next turn.
- **View mode** (V) moves a cursor over the map instead of a unit, to look around without giving orders. M, or a click on one of your units, returns to move mode.
- **Go to** (G) sends a unit on a journey of several turns. It goes on by itself each turn until it arrives; press Esc while choosing the square to give up.
- **Zoom.** The map has four zoom levels. Z and X, the mouse wheel, a trackpad pinch, or the browser's own zoom keys (Ctrl or Cmd with plus and minus) all change it.
- **Pop-up questions** always have a cautious answer, and Esc chooses it.
- The same key map is kept in the repository as `docs/KEYS.md`.
