**New World** is a turn-based game of founding colonies in the New World and leading them to independence. You sail from Europe in 1492 with a ship, a soldier and a pioneer. You explore an unknown coast, found colonies and put their people to work in the fields and the workshops, and trade with Europe and with the native peoples. As the colonies grow, their people come to want a say in their own affairs. In time you declare independence and fight the Crown's army for it. At the end the game is scored, and the best games go into a Hall of Fame.

**[Play New World](https://colebrumley.github.io/new-world/)**

## What you need

- **A browser.** The game is a web page made of static files. There is no account to make, nothing to download and nothing to install. It is free.
- **The latest Chrome, Firefox or Safari.** The game is made for these three.
- **A window at least 1024 by 640.** That is the smallest it is made for; 1280 by 800 or larger is comfortable. It plays with a keyboard and a mouse (or a trackpad).
- **No connection, once it has loaded.** After the first visit the browser keeps every file the game needs, so it starts and plays with the network gone. Your browser can also install it as an app.

Your games are kept in the browser you play in. See [[Help and Options]] for how saving works and how to copy a game to a file.
