**New World** is a turn-based game of founding colonies in the New World and leading them to independence. You sail from Europe with a handful of colonists, explore an unknown coast, found colonies and put their people to work, trade with Europe and with the native peoples, and in time declare independence and fight the Crown for it. It is free and plays in the browser.

**[Play New World](https://colebrumley.github.io/new-world/)**
